## I have an opinion

If I learned anything from our experimental DHF shenanigans, its that everyone has an opinion.  You don't have to like it, you might not agree with it, you probably don't have time to rebutt "all of it" (pick your battles), but now I'm on that DHF drip with @acidyo and I am set out to prove one of my opinions - "making posts about what you (we) do is important, especially in "development", and not doing so - considering HIVE's nature - is a sociological crime".

Yes posting is hard - and its a separate skill set you might say than "development".  

And beyond that - development is non-linear.  It turns out - Google has an opinion too!

## Claude, I have a problem - and I need your help

So the first thing you can do when "things happen" is talk to your agent.  Talk to your agent and make a plan - this is the way.  The modern way:

1. Please explain this, what is the scope?
2. Let's make a plan to address this - what are the options?
3. What are the tradeoffs - pros and cons?

And soon enough you will be "proceeding step by step":
